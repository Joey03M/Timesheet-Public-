Privacy Policy

OneStop CNC Timesheets is a private, internal timesheet and pay-tracking application. It is not a public product.

What it accesses

The app connects to a single Google account to:

  • Create draft emails in Gmail (via the gmail.compose scope) — it can only create drafts, it cannot read, send, or delete existing email.
  • Upload and manage timesheet/payslip files in a dedicated folder in Google Drive (via the drive.file scope) — it can only see files it creates itself, not your wider Drive.

What it does not do

This data is never shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party. It is used solely to generate and store the account holder's own timesheets and payslips.
